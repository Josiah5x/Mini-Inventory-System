Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Place an Order

Do you have all products physically in stock?

Stock varies by product. Some items may be available immediately, while others may need to be sourced. Contact us to confirm availability before making payment.

How do I check if a spare part fits my machine?

Provide the machine or vehicle make, model, serial number where applicable, and original part number. Photos and dimensions can also help us check compatibility.

Can you deliver outside Abuja?

Delivery to other states can be discussed subject to the item's availability, carrier arrangements, freight cost, and destination.

Can I inspect equipment before purchase?

Ask us about inspection arrangements for the particular product. Inspection depends on the item's location and the supplier's conditions.

Do you sell genuine OEM and aftermarket parts?

Product type and origin should be confirmed for each item. Ask us to specify whether a quotation is for a genuine OEM part, an aftermarket part, or a compatible alternative.

How do I receive a quotation?

Complete the enquiry form above and continue to WhatsApp. Review your message and send it to our business number. We can then respond based on the details you provide.
